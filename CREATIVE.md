# Creative decisions — gta6-clipper-01

## The moment (to be fixed after watching the trailer)

Source: GTA 6 Trailer 2, Rockstar's official trailer
(https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VQRLujxTm3c), fetched in this session.
One moment, not a montage. Candidates in Trailer 2: the opening
Lucia/Jason beat, the Vice City flyover, the car-chase / heist action, the
beach and street chaos shots. The pick goes to whichever single beat has the
most motion and the clearest "what just happened" in its first two seconds —
that is what holds a Shorts viewer past the swipe.

Why it will hold a viewer: Trailer 2 is the freshest official GTA 6 footage
there is, it dropped the same week as this bounty, and everyone searching
GTA 6 right now wants exactly this — the new Vice City, the new characters,
the action. Opening on the action instead of a title card means the viewer
is already inside the moment before they can decide to swipe.

## Hook

First seconds open directly on the action beat — no intro card, no logo, no
"watch this". The footage does the hooking.

## Pitch (on-screen text, end of clip)

Line 1: "Make clips like this with AI"
Line 2: "and earn money on make.fun"
Line 3: "make.fun/bounty/gta6-clipper-01"

Each line stays on screen at least 2–3 seconds, inside the vertical safe
area, clear of YouTube's buttons at the bottom and the right edge. No voice
track: no TTS tool is installed on this machine, and the bounty allows text
on screen alone.

## Title / tags

Title: "GTA 6 Trailer 2 — The Chase That Broke Vice City" (adjusted to the
moment actually picked). Tags: #GTA6 #Shorts #GTA6Trailer #ViceCity
#makefun. Description carries the bounty's line word for word plus the play
code — see `upload-package.md`.

## Target

Top 20 of the paid places (1st $100, 2nd $70, 3rd $50, 4th–10th $20,
11th–20th $14). Places go by views at the deadline (23 Nov 08:00 WIB),
most first. The clip is built to earn its views from search + Shorts feed
traffic on GTA 6 hype, not from any bought or botted viewing.
